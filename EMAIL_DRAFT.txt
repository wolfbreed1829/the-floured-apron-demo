Subject: A fresh website idea for The Floured Apron

Hi!

We put together a working website concept for The Floured Apron and wanted to share it with you:

https://wolfbreed1829.github.io/the-floured-apron-demo/

It gives the bakery a warmer, more visual storefront, makes the menu easier to browse on a phone, and lets you try putting together an order. You can also click “Try the content studio” to experiment with the homepage wording, prices, and availability.

This is only a demo—nothing is sent or charged. The photos are placeholders for your own baking, and the content studio saves changes only in your browser.

We’d love to hear what feels right for your bakery and what you’d change.
